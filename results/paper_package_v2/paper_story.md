# Core scientific story

Within-radar HAR on official plotted spectrograms looks strong (0.843 / 0.898 / 0.922).
Cross-radar transfer does not (24→77 0.039).
Plotting is a confound, not the cause (three-radar Radar-ID stays 1.000 after crop).
Numerical Doppler still carries three-radar identity (0.993).
On the 24/77 GHz pair only, velocity alignment is a weak contributor (1.000 → 0.939; pair separation almost unchanged).
Harmonized STFT/MTI and simple calibration fail the stated gates.
Adapters and motion factors keep activity and radar information together.
Part of the paired velocity-marginal response is motion-conditioned (24→77 $R^2$ 0.744),
but empirical 2D translation hits a low TEST oracle (0.418).
The public pairs cannot separate motion, sensor, geometry and subject.
Next resource is controlled data, not a deeper translator.

Acquisition Toolkit is Outlook only. Mock recordings are not results.
