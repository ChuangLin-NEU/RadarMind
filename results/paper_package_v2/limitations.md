# Limitations (must stay in the paper)

- 10 GHz effective carrier is unresolved; no common velocity axis with 24/77.
- 24/77 physical range axes are not jointly recoverable from the used pipelines.
- Per-acquisition distance, aspect and body orientation are unavailable.
- Subject and environment identifiers are incomplete relative to the 327 timestamps.
- 327 paired acquisitions, 7 actions, one reported laboratory setting.
- Official public representations are heterogeneous plotted images.
- Rank-20 2D empirical translation has a low TEST oracle ($R^2$ 0.418); that oracle is an upper bound, not deployment accuracy.
- Cross-environment transfer is not tested.
- Adapter and calibration experiments are known-radar unless explicitly labeled LORO (supplementary). LORO is DG; CORAL is UDA.
